# 🌾 PIKRAKSHA (पीकरक्षा)
### AI Early Warning for Crop Pests, Diseases and Weather-Linked Threats — Before the Damage
**Seva First Innovation Challenge 2026 • Maharashtra Zone**  
**Category:** Women Category • **Sector:** Agriculture  
**Sub-Theme:** Early detection of crop pests, diseases and weather-linked crop threats

---

## 🌟 Overview
**PIKRAKSHA** is an AI-powered, farm-level early warning system designed for smallholder farmers, women farmers, and agriculture officers across Maharashtra.

Instead of waiting for visible crop damage and reacting with repeated chemical sprays, PIKRAKSHA combines **4 real-time inputs**:
1. **Local Weather Forecasts & Microclimate** (Mahavedh Automatic Weather Stations & IMD).
2. **Crop Growth Stage & Phenology Calendar** (Sowing date & days after sowing).
3. **Pheromone & Sticky Trap Counts** (Enforcing official Economic Threshold Levels - ETL).
4. **Crop Photographs & Observed Symptoms** (MobileNet deep learning plant pathology scanner).

## 🚀 Key Innovations & Features

### 1. Dual Portal Architecture
* **👨‍🌾 Farmer Portal**:
  * **Dashboard**: Farm overview, high-risk alert banner, 4 KPI cards, 7-day risk bars, and trap trend.
  * **7-Day Threat Timeline**: Central day-by-day predictive risk forecast with interactive "What-If" weather anomaly simulator.
  * **Why Am I at Risk? (Explainable AI)**: Factor-by-factor breakdown (Pheromone trap count, crop stage, night temperature, village swarm density) with score contribution rather than black-box AI.
  * **Daily Farmer Action Plan**: Interactive prioritized IPM task checklist with persistence.
  * **AI Crop Scanner**: Photo diagnosis with observed symptom verification checklist & attention heatmap overlay.
  * **Pheromone Traps & 20-Boll Scouting Calculator**: Configurable 8-moth / 3-day consecutive threshold rule and 5% ETL random sampling calculator.
  * **Precision Weather & Spray Window**: Hourly Delta-T evaporation risk, wind drift guard, and honeybee pollinator protection hours.
  * **Village Swarm Radar**: 2 km community ring-fence alert connecting neighboring farm traps.
  * **Treatment Follow-Up**: Tracks pre- and post-intervention pest counts to verify recovery.
  * **KisanAI Voice Copilot**: 2-way oral voice assistant in Marathi, Hindi, and English for non-literate farmers.

* **🏛️ Agriculture Officer Portal**:
  * **Maharashtra Overview & District Command**: Surveillance telemetry across Yavatmal, Nashik, Pune, Latur, Solapur, and Kolhapur.
  * **Trap ETL Surveillance**: Real-time identification of talukas crossing economic thresholds.
  * **Expert Verification Desk**: Agronomists audit and certify farmer-submitted AI diagnoses.
  * **Advisory Broadcasting**: Mass regional broadcast simulator via SMS, WhatsApp, and automated voice calls.
  * **Impact Analytics**: Financial loss averted modeling (₹3.75 Cr projected across pilot) and 3 commitments tracker.
